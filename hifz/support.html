Common questions

How do I sign in? Enter your email address. We send you a sign-in link; open it on the same phone.

How do I connect with my teacher? Open the Teaching screen. Your teacher can then see your hifz record; either of you can remove the link at any time.

How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Settings and tap "Delete account". This permanently removes your account and all of your data. If you no longer have the app, email us from the address you signed in with and we will delete it for you.

Can I keep a copy of my data? Yes. Settings has an export that downloads everything as a file.
