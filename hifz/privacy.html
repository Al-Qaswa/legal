Tibyan privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026 · Applies to the Tibyan app

Tibyan helps you plan and keep track of memorising the Qur'an, on your own or with a teacher. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

What we collect

You can look around Tibyan without an account. To keep a hifz record, you sign in, and your progress is kept in your account so it is there on any device you sign in on. For a signed-in account we store:

  • Your email address, to sign you in. We email you a sign-in link; there is no password.
  • A display name, only if you choose to set one.
  • Your hifz record: the portions you memorise and revise, your sessions, plans, schedules and any breaks you log.
  • Your time zone, so that days and schedules line up with your own.
  • Teacher and student links, if you connect with a teacher or student. A teacher you connect with can see your hifz record until either of you removes the link.

There are no advertisements, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone for their own use.

Where it is kept

Your account is stored in a database in Frankfurt, Germany, run by our hosting providers (Render for the server, Neon for the database). Sign-in emails are sent through Resend. These providers process the information only to run the service for us. Information is sent over an encrypted connection.

Your choices

  • Export: in Settings you can download all of your data as a file.
  • Delete: in Settings, "Delete account" permanently removes your account and everything stored with it, and ends any teacher's access. You can also ask us to delete it by email (see below).

Children

Tibyan is meant for students of the Qur'an of any age, often with a teacher. A child should use it with a parent's or teacher's permission. We only collect what is listed above, and a parent can ask us to delete a child's account at any time.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what happens to your information, we will update this policy before that version is released, and change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request to delete your data: see the support page.
