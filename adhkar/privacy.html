Adhkar privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026 · Applies to the Adhkar app (shown on the home screen as Sakinah)

Adhkar is a book of supplications with a counter. This page explains what the app does with information about you. In short: in its current version, we do not collect any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The current version of the app does not send information about you, your device or how you use the app to us or to anyone else. There are no advertisements, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves some things in your device's own storage:

  • your reading preferences, such as text size, language, theme and whether translation or transliteration is shown;
  • your counts, bookmarks, lists and the days you completed a sitting;
  • an email address, only if you choose to enter one on the optional sign-in screen.

This information is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time from the app's settings, or by deleting the app.

Links to other websites

Each supplication shows its source, and some sources link to websites such as sunnah.com or quran.com. These open in your browser, and those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Future versions of the app may add features that use the internet, such as syncing your lists between devices. If that changes what happens to your information, we will update this policy before that version is released, and change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the support page.
