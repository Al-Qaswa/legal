Common questions

How do I count? Tap anywhere on the supplication's card. The number counts down to zero, then the next one opens.

How do I hear the recitation? Before starting the morning or evening sitting, choose "Read all - auto turn" under Audio. The page turns by itself when each recitation ends.

How do I make my own list? On the home screen, tap "Make a list from the book", then add supplications from any collection.

I found a mistake in a text or a source. Please email us with the supplication and what looks wrong. Accuracy matters more to us than anything else in the app.
