Common questions

Does my child need an account? No. Stories can be read without signing in. Sign in if you want your child's stars and progress kept.

How do I sign in? Tap the person icon, answer the grown-up question, and enter your email address. We send you a sign-in link; open it on the same device.

Can more than one child use it? Yes. Add a reader for each child; each keeps their own progress and story language.

How do I delete my account? In the app, open the reader's page, scroll to "For grown-ups" and tap "Delete account". This permanently removes the account, every reader on it and all their progress. If you no longer have the app, email us from the address you signed in with and we will delete it for you.
