Qaswa privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026 · Applies to the Qaswa app (Al-Qaswa stories for children)

Qaswa is a reading app for children: illustrated stories of the prophets, with narration and short quizzes. It is made for children to use with a parent. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

Without an account

A child can open and read stories without anyone signing in. We do not ask a child for a name, an email address or anything else to do this.

With a parent's account

A parent can sign in so that a child's reading and stars are kept. Signing in is behind a question meant for grown-ups. For a signed-in account we store:

  • The parent's email address, to sign in. We email a sign-in link; there is no password.
  • Readers: for each child the parent adds, a first name or nickname, the picture they chose from the app's own set, and the language they read in.
  • Reading progress: which stories each reader has started or finished, the page they reached, and their quiz answers and stars.

A reader's progress screen shows one number comparing them with other readers ("ahead of N% of readers"). It is worked out from totals only. No other child's name, picture or score is ever shown, and there is no leaderboard.

There are no advertisements, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. We do not sell any information or share it with anyone for their own use.

Where it is kept

Accounts are stored by our hosting providers (Render for the server, Neon for the database). Sign-in emails are sent through Resend. Story pictures and narration are loaded from our storage, currently Google Firebase Storage, which sees the request but not who is reading. These providers process information only to run the service for us. Information is sent over an encrypted connection.

Deleting an account

In the app, open the reader's page, scroll to "For grown-ups" and tap "Delete account". After the grown-up question and a confirmation, the account, every reader on it and all their progress are permanently removed. You can also ask us by email (see below).

Children

Qaswa is for children, used with a parent. We collect only what is listed above, only once a parent has signed in, and a parent can delete it at any time.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what happens to your information, we will update this policy before that version is released, and change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request to delete data: see the support page.
