Common questions

A prayer time looks wrong for my city. Check the calculation method in Settings first; different regions follow different methods. If it still looks wrong, email us your city and the time you expected.

How do I mark a prayer? On the Today screen, tap the prayer to record it.

Where is my history? Tap the calendar icon at the top of the Today screen to see your record.
