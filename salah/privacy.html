Salah privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026 · Applies to the Salah app

Salah shows the prayer times for your city and helps you keep a record of your prayers. This page explains what the app does with information about you. In short: in its current version, we do not collect any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The current version of the app does not send information about you, your device or how you use the app to us or to anyone else. There are no advertisements, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not use your device's location; you choose your city yourself.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves some things in your device's own storage:

  • the city you chose and your settings, such as the calculation method and time format;
  • your record of prayers, including prayers you are making up (qada).

This information is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or deleting the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Future versions of the app may add an optional account to keep your record across devices. If that changes what happens to your information, we will update this policy before that version is released, and change the date at the top of this page.
